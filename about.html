A pie-in-the-sky idea
from 1978.

Flying Pie launched as, well, a pie in the sky, and grew into a much-beloved homegrown institution with hot spots across Boise, Meridian, Eagle and Nampa. Our pizzas go in a box, but we think outside it (and inside it too, because that's where the delicious lives). That out-of-the-box mindset is what made us whirled famous.

The flight log

Four-plus decades of unreasonably amazing pizza and unreasonably awesome memories.

1985

Wheels up to Nampa

Our first location outside Ada County. The flying part of Flying Pie starts to feel literal.

1987

Pizza school is in session

We started teaching pizza-making know-how in Treasure Valley community education classes.

1991

Our dough goes interstate

Flying Pie dough lands on supermarket shelves in six states.

1992

Special delivery: Alaska

By car, helicopter, plane, then puddle jumper, we delivered a pizza to Alaska just to make someone's day.

1993

Gourmet Night begins

We started getting experimentally delicious, one wild special at a time.

1994

The millionth delivery

A limo, a marching band, and a Pizza-for-Life prize surprised the lucky soul who placed our millionth home delivery order. Same year, we served the double-habanero pie for the very first time.

2002

It's Your Day premieres

We started welcoming customers into our kitchen to make their own pizza, for free. Still going.

2004

Flora is born

A giant ball of foil enters the world. Long may she gleam.

2009

Man v. Food drops in

The Travel Channel stopped by to try our habanero pie. No sweat. (Okay, a little sweat.)

2010

Best in the state

USA Today named Flying Pie the best pizza in Idaho.

2013

Hello, Meridian

Lucky location number three opens its doors.

2016

Back to Broadway

We brought Flying Pie back to the east end of town on Broadway Avenue.

2018

40 years & a beer wall

We opened Nampa with a 28-tap self-serve beer wall and celebrated 40 years of unreasonably amazing pizza.

2020

Overland opens

A new hot spot joins the family on West Overland Road.

2022

Lucky number seven

We opened our seventh store in Eagle to much fanfare. The flock keeps growing.

Almost every day, somebody eats free

It's Your Day!

A few lucky people get to step into our kitchen and build their own 10" specialty or custom pizza, totally free. It all depends on what the "Name of the Day" is. Bring a valid ID, come hungry, and check the schedule to see when your name comes up.

Daily windows run 2–4 PM and 8–10 PM, for 5–20 folks per shift. You can even bring one friend into the kitchen with you.
